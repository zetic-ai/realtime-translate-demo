# Brand assets

Bouygues Speak and Bouygues Read are Bouygues Telecom typefaces. These files were sourced from the official Trilogy v4 assets at:

`https://assets.bouyguestelecom.fr/TRILOGY/trilogy-styles@4/default/assets/fonts/`

The `bouygues-speak.woff2`, `bouygues-read-regular.woff2` and `bouygues-read-medium.woff2` files were converted to TTF for native registration and are shared with the Aperçu iOS demo. Font names are unchanged. These brand assets remain subject to their owners' terms and are not relicensed by this repository. The ZETIC logo remains a ZETIC brand asset.
